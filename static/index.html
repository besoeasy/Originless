    Shared canvas

    A collaborative 64 × 64 pixel board. Pick a color, then click or drag to paint. Every painted pixel becomes an Ed25519-signed event and is broadcast live to everyone.

    How the canvas works

    Your browser signs each pixel action locally, posts it to /events, and listens to /events/stream. The canvas replays the last 30 days of pixel events from oldest to newest; the newest event wins for each cell.
